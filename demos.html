Why we ask for your email. Sorry for the extra step. Several of these demos run live AI models, and every question costs real money. An open page would let bots and spammers spend it within days. So you sign in once with your email address, and that one sign-in covers the demos for about 24 hours. Nobody has to approve you. 2 · What the market knows skills-based job matching

A market is built on what it knows. Here are three services over one body of knowledge: the wiki itself, a question page over it, and a guide written from it. The market is skills-based job matching, worked through one specific case: Indigenous workers and the employers who want to hire them.

Why this example. Matching what a worker can do to what a job needs is a general problem. Adding the Indigenous dimension makes it specific, and a specific case is more useful for diagnosis and for learning than a general one. The idea came from casual conversations with people who know the field. Nobody commissioned it: it is DeeperPoint’s own construction. Everything in these demos is drawn solely from public sources, and none of it speaks for any Indigenous person, community or organization.

Before the demo opens

We ask for your email address first, to stop automated programs running up our AI costs. The sign-in screen comes from Cloudflare, a security company we use. There is no password to create.

  1. Type your email address and press Send login code.
  2. Open the email from Cloudflare (noreply@notify.cloudflare.com). Check your spam folder if you do not see it.
  3. Reading email on this device? Press the orange Log in button.
    Reading it elsewhere, such as on your phone? Type the six numbers into the page where you started.

The code lasts 10 minutes. If the page says it was already used, press Request new code.
