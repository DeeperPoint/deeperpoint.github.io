MarketForge

The Procedural Marketplace Creation Roadmap

Building a successful coordination marketplace requires a clean architectural split. MarketForge provides the structured, step-by-step engineering roadmap that takes a target vertical from diagnostic scoping through local simulation and launch.

⬇ Download Creation Guide (PDF) Explore ForgeTwin Sandbox →

Core Infrastructure vs. Domain Customization

To ensure scalable and rapid deployments, DeeperPoint divides the building blocks of a vertical marketplace into two clear layers:

🛠️ Core Platform Layer (DeeperPoint Open-Source) Delivers generic, reusable underlying matching algorithms (Cosolvent), reference knowledge models (CommonContext), default prompts, and automated metadata feedback loops.
👤 Domain Customization Layer (Vertical Founder) Sours regional legal documents, configures specialized attributes (`marketplace.yaml`), vibe-codes the user interface layout, calibrates matching stories, and operates curation feedback channels.

Generic Participant Roles

DeeperPoint operates on a highly generic, extensible role schema designed to fit any complex transaction corridor:

  • Supply: Providers, caregivers, sellers, or fabricators.
  • Demand: Consumers, recipients, buyers, or clients.
  • Facilitators: Intermediaries, inspectors, brokers, or logistics managers.

Validation Sandbox

The Digital Twin Architecture

Before launching to live users, the vertical founder constructs a local **Digital Twin** modeling the industry vertical. This integrates your custom YAML parameters and custom-designed synthetic user cohorts directly into the matching protocol environment:

  • Standard Cohort: Verifies that fully compliant counterparties match immediately.
  • Treatment Cohort (e.g. "Dave" Persona): Proves that uncertified local providers carrying alternative compliance triggers successfully bypass rigid blockers.
  • Control Cohort: Confirms that unqualified providers are safely blocked and routed to dynamic data-enrichment queries.

CommonContext Continuous Curation Loops

Loop 1: Initial Calibration

Occurs during the sandbox setup phase. The sponsor curator feeds regulatory manuals into the text parsers, validates proposed vector schemas, and seeds Alternative Compliance Rules.

Result: Calibrates matching models to regional industrial realities.

Loop 2: Profile Enrichment

Occurs in real-time. When a transaction is blocked solely because a specific workaround parameter is unknown, the AI automatically prompts the participant (via SMS or WhatsApp) and dynamically enriches their profile.

Result: Unlocks blocked matches in minutes without manual support.

Loop 3: Curator Pull Loop

Occurs during operations. When a match is blocked by a standard lacking any workaround rule, the system alerts the curator's Gap Signal UI, prompting them to crawl and approve a new ACR.

Result: System gets progressively smarter with every match.
