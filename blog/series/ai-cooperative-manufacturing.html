New Frontier: AI Powered Cooperative Manufacturing

The Ontario Roadmap showed that independent firms can cooperate as a virtual mega-factory. But the unit of assembly was always the whole firm. Cooperative Specialization goes deeper — extending the same AI-brokered coordination tools to departments, machines, and individual specialists inside manufacturing companies. The structural waste it is designed to correct is enormous, and it begins with a simple physical fact: most productive capabilities come in lumps.
