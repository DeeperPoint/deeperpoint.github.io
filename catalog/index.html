Catalog of Market Ideas

274 ideas for thin markets that nobody has built yet, across 29 sectors

Each entry is an idea: a thin market that the right matching infrastructure could open up. Any single idea can be argued with. The point is how many plausible ones there are, across unrelated sectors, places and kinds of participant. Click a title to read its summary.

These are ideas, not built markets. None of the marketplaces described here exists yet. The story attached to each idea is fiction: its people and firms are invented to show how a match could work, and the market figures are estimates.

Why ideas, and so many of them? The best evidence for this approach would be a collection of working, commercial marketplaces built with it. Assembling that would be costly and would take years. Working the approach through hundreds of different markets on paper was the method we could afford now: a low-cost check that the same reasoning holds up across unrelated sectors, each idea set out in enough detail to be argued with.
